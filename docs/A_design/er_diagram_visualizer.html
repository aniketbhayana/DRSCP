Enhanced ER (EER) Concepts Implemented in DRSCP

1. Disjoint Role Specialization:

app_users is the superclass entity that specializes into Agency Manager (agency_id), Volunteer (volunteer_id), and Requester (requester_id) with exclusive role constraints.

2. Polymorphic Allocation Specialization:

allocations targets at least one resource: Shelter Bed (shelter_id), Responder Dispatch (volunteer_id), or Inventory Supply (resource_type_id), validated by trigger.

3. ACID Trigger Synchronization:

Denormalized triage column priority_score and bed counts current_occupancy are mathematically updated by PostgreSQL triggers on every transaction.
